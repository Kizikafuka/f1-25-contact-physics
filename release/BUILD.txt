How to build kizi.ai-tweaks (Contact Physics) from this source
============================================
Needs Zig 0.17 (https://ziglang.org) and the Swofty SDK headers (packages/sdk/include from
https://github.com/Swofty-Developments/SwoftyLauncher, MIT).

  zig c++ -target x86_64-windows-gnu -std=c++23 -O2 -shared -s -I<sdk include> -I common ^
      -o kizi_ai_tweaks.dll native\src\ai_tweaks.cpp res\dll.rc -lkernel32

native\src\ai_tweaks.cpp      the in-game part (softening, reaction time, punctures, hotkey, settings for the physics)
native\src\osd.hpp            on-screen messages (the game's race notification)
common\kizi_contact_sim.hpp   the contact physics (hook at the end of the game's contact code, rigid-body impulses)
common\kizi_mem.hpp           small memory helpers
